Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information may be provided through this portfolio and how it is handled.

Information You Provide

When you contact me through the contact form on this portfolio, you may provide information such as your name, email address and message.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the contact form.

How Your Information Is Used

Information submitted through the contact form is used primarily to read and respond to your inquiry, discuss potential projects or opportunities, and communicate with you when necessary.

I do not sell personal information submitted through this portfolio.

Contact Form

This portfolio uses Formspree to process contact form submissions. Information you submit through the form is therefore transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver your message.

Formspree operates under its own privacy practices and policies.

External Links & Services

This website may contain links to external platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram and WhatsApp.

When you follow an external link, you leave this portfolio. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies and data practices, which are outside my control.

Cookies & Tracking

This portfolio does not intentionally use its own advertising cookies or tracking technologies to build advertising profiles of visitors.

However, third-party services or external websites you choose to visit may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their policies.

Data Security

Reasonable care is taken when handling information received through this portfolio. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, contact methods or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, you can contact me through the contact section of my portfolio.
